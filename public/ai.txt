# StudyShare AI Access Policy
# https://studyshare.in

## Allowed Use
AI assistants and search crawlers may index and summarize public pages:
- Homepage (https://studyshare.in/)
- Blog posts (https://studyshare.in/blog/*)
- Mobile app page (https://studyshare.in/mobile-app)
- Legal pages (privacy-policy, terms-of-use, community-guidelines, account-deletion)

## Disallowed Use
- Do not index or summarize authenticated pages requiring login:
  /study, /notices, /chatroom, /profile, /messages, /explore, /bookmarks, /ai-chat
- Do not use user-generated content for training without explicit consent
- Do not impersonate StudyShare or its users

## Contact
For questions about AI access: support@studyshare.in
